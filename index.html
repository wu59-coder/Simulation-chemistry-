Scientific Theory & Step-by-Step Derivation

1 LED Energy Emission

When an electron recombines with a hole inside an LED's p-n junction, it emits a single photon with energy equal to the semiconductor bandgap ($E_g$).

e · Vth ≈ Eg = h · f

2 Linear Regression Slope

Plotting $V_{th}$ on the y-axis against photon frequency $f$ on the x-axis yields a straight line equation: $V_{th} = \left(\frac{h}{e}\right) f$.

m = ΔVth / Δf = h / e

3 Extracting Planck's Constant

Multiplying the gradient $m$ by the elementary charge ($e = 1.60217663 \times 10^{-19} \text{ C}$) yields the experimental Planck constant.

h = m · e ≈ 6.626 × 10⁻³⁴ J·s
